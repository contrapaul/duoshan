Rules
Hit an opponent with a live ball (not yet bounced) to knock them out.
Catch a live ball to knock out the thrower and bring back a teammate.
Holding a ball lets you block (hold right click or E to raise it as a shield); empty hands let you catch (click or E). Q throws too.
A target appears every 90 s: hit it for 12 s of slow motion and 100 Dodgecoins.
Glowing balls are live. Heavy (blue) balls can't be caught, and they knock a shield out of your hands. Speed (orange) balls still trip after bouncing.
Cross the yellow centerline and you're out.
